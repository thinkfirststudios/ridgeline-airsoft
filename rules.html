02 / Eye protection

Eye protection.

Eye protection standard

ANSI Z87.1

The one rule with a named standard. "Shatterproof" on its own does not meet it.

  1. Fully sealed goggles at all times by everyone on the field of play.
  2. Must be shatterproof and no less than ANSI Z87.1 rated.
  3. Wire mesh goggles are not allowed except with shatterproof eye protection worn underneath.
  4. Full face protection is always required for players under the age of 18, and recommended for everyone.
