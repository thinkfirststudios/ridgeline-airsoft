Fortress

One team defends a fort on a timer with a set number of revives. The attackers have unlimited revives.

Mode 07 / 08

Faction

It starts as a free-for-all. When a player is hit they drop — heal them for 10 seconds and they join your faction. You don't have to be the one who shot them, just the first to reach them.
